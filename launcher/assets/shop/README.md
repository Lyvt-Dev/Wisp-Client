Shop items and card art, generated by
`cargo run -p wisp-cosmetics --example shop_items -- launcher/assets/shop`.
The `.json` files are cosmetic items (docs/cosmetics.md); every picture is drawn from
Wisp's own pixel art, with no Minecraft assets.

`founders_cape_texture.png` is the owner's own Founders Cape artwork (a 256×128 HD cape in
vanilla's layout); the generator halves it onto the shop's 2× cape box.

The Wisp Original Cape draws Wisp's own mark from `crates/wisp-theme/icons/wisp-256.png`.
The emote cards (Dark Ascension, Bruh, L Dance, Celestial Wings) are the generator's posed
wearer with the emote's effects painted round them; the older emotes' cards are game screenshots.

`zedix_cape_logo.png` is the owner's Zedix logo (a purple Z); the generator draws it onto the
Zedix Cape and animates the light over it.
