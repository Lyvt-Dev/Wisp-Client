# Wisp for Android 0.15.1

The first Wisp app for phones: Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3 in Rust, on Android.

**Install:** download `Wisp-Client-0.15.1-android-arm64.apk` on your phone and open it (Android
asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager). Android 10 or newer, 64-bit
(arm64), Vulkan or OpenGL ES 3.

**Set up (once):**
1. **Sign in with Microsoft** in the app: it shows a code; open microsoft.com/link, enter it and
   sign in with the account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.
2. **Get the game files from your PC.** The game files come from your own Minecraft and can't be
   in the app. On your PC, update the Wisp launcher to 0.15.1 (`Wisp-Client-0.15.1-Setup-x64.exe`
   on this page), press Play once there if you never have, then open **Settings › Phone ›
   Connect a phone**. In the app choose **Get them from my PC**, pick your PC and type the code.
   Phone and PC need the same Wi-Fi; about 800 MB are copied.
3. Press **Play**.

**Touch controls:** a joystick where your left thumb lands (push it to the edge to sprint), swipe
on the right to look, tap to use or place, hold still to break or attack. Buttons for jump, sneak
(tap to latch), inventory and drop beside the hotbar; pause, chat, perspective and the Wisp Menu
(the star) at the top left.
Tap a hotbar slot to pick it. In menus a tap clicks, holding still right-clicks and dragging
scrolls; the back button works like Escape. Text fields open the phone's keyboard. A Bluetooth
keyboard works too.

**Cosmetics and the Store** come with the game as on the PC: tap the star for the Wisp Menu with
the Store, your coins, capes, wings, pets, trails, auras and emotes on the Wisp server, friends
and modules. They need the Microsoft sign-in.

**Known limits of this first version:**
- Controllers over Bluetooth aren't read yet; a keyboard is.
- Voice chat needs the microphone permission, which this version doesn't ask for: allow it for
  Wisp in Android's app settings to talk.
- The phone keeps no system clipboard: text copied in the game pastes in the game.
- With gesture navigation, a tap right at the bottom edge of a menu can be missed: tap a little
  higher on the button.
- Tested in the Android emulator (setup, copying the game files from a PC, sign-in code, the
  game, a new world, touch controls, background and back), not yet on a real phone: tell us how
  it runs on yours.
