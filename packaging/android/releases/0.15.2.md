# Wisp for Android 0.15.2

Faster on phones, no more closing the app by accident, and a new home screen.

**Install:** download `Wisp-Client-0.15.2-android-arm64.apk` on your phone and open it; it
installs over 0.15.1 and keeps your game files, worlds and sign-in.

- **Runs much smoother.** The phone now starts with settings made for it: the world is drawn at
  half the screen's resolution (the menus and the HUD stay sharp), fast graphics, render distance
  6, no clouds, shadows or smooth lighting, at most 60 FPS, and battery mode, so the phone stays
  cool and doesn't slow down after a few minutes. Chunks load in smaller pieces per frame, so
  walking into new terrain doesn't stutter. They're set once with this update; change anything
  in Options afterwards and it stays.
- **The app no longer closes when your thumb slides off the screen.** Wisp now hides Android's
  status and navigation bars: a swipe from the edge only shows them for a moment instead of going
  home. The back button on the home screen doesn't close the app anymore either.
- **Buttons moved away from the edges.** Jump and sneak sit higher, pause sits apart from chat,
  perspective and the Wisp Menu, every button has a light rim so it shows on snow and sand, and
  the joystick shows faintly where it rests.
- **The joystick walks the right way:** pushing it left walks left (it was mirrored).
- **New home screen:** the Wisp feather and a big Play button on the left, your account and the
  game files as cards on the right.
