# Wisp Launcher 0.10.11

- Emotes. Press B in game to open the emote wheel, move the mouse to pick one and click to play
  it. The first emote is the Black Hole: a black hole tears open above you, you float up and orbit
  it while the world bends around it, its glowing disk swirls and matter streams in, then it
  collapses in a white flash and a shock wave. Moving cancels it. Only you see it.
- The Shop has an Emotes tab. The Black Hole costs 600 Wisp Coins; once bought it is in your
  wheel the next time you play. Emotes not bought show locked in the wheel.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
