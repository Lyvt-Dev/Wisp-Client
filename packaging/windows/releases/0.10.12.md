# Wisp Launcher 0.10.12

- The Black Hole emote is calm and relaxed now. The hole opens slowly, and you float up weightless,
  lying on your back. You drift slowly around it, turning, rolling and swaying with your arms and
  legs moving as if in water, with stars hanging in the air around you. At the end it fades away
  with a soft ripple and sets you down. It lasts about 15 seconds. There is no more shaking,
  flash or explosion.
- Fixed a crash at start with a resource pack on: on some PCs Windows refused to let the game
  open the folder links in the resource pack cache, so the fonts failed to load (os error 448,
  "untrusted mount point"). The game now checks the links and copies the folders where they
  don't open, and rebuilds an old cache that has such links.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
