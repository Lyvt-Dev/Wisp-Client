# Wisp Launcher 0.10.13

- New in the Shop: **Daylight** (800 coins), a sword by @gold3nz that you carry across your back,
  hilt over your right shoulder. It sits in the new Weapons category and goes with any cape.
- New in the Shop: **Nebula Wings** (1,500 coins), wings of drifting violet and pink nebula that
  beat slowly, glow in the dark and shed stardust. They are worn on the back, so they take the
  place of a cape.
- New emote: **Supernova Crown** (800 coins). A star gathers over your head, swells and bursts in
  a flash and a shock wave, then settles into a turning crown of light inside a fading nebula.
  Press B and pick it from the wheel.
- The Shop is tidier: the Featured tab groups items by category, each with a link to its tab.
  The owned count sits beside the star in the top bar.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
