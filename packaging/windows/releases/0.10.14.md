# Wisp Launcher 0.10.14

- **Supernova Crown** is rebuilt from scratch. A real star now gathers over your head, with a
  boiling surface and a corona that darkens the world around it. It collapses, bursts in a flash
  and a shock wave, and throws off a glowing shell of red, blue and gold gas with a pulsar at its
  heart. Its light settles on your head as a turning crown.
- **Nebula Wings** look much better: real spread wings of layered, shaded feathers made of night
  sky, from deep blue at the arm to tips that glow cyan white. Each wing bends at the wrist as it
  beats.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
