# Wisp Launcher 0.10.9

- Trails now show in the game. The Stardust Trail leaves soft violet dust and white sparkles
  behind you as you move; the Comet Trail draws an icy blue tail along your path. Both glow at
  night.
- New in the Shop: auras, effects all around you. The Storm Aura crackles with lightning, the
  Starlight Orbit circles your waist with a ring of golden stars, and the Nebula Aura spirals
  pink and violet stardust up around you. They glow at night too.
- New pet: Astra the Cosmic Fox, a fox of deep space that sits on your shoulder, with starlit
  fur, cyan eyes, nebula-pink ears and a tail that ends in a glowing star.
- Nova the Star Owl is redrawn: its head and wings no longer float apart from its body, and it
  has a starry night coat, a pale face with amber eyes, tilted ear tufts and gold talons.
- Pets are bigger, glow in the dark and shed glowing sparkles.
- Capes are now HD and animated, at 4× Minecraft's cape resolution. The Founders Cape shows its
  original artwork at full resolution, with a soft light sweeping across it, its star breathing
  and small stars twinkling. The Nebula Cape's nebula drifts and the Aurora Cape's lights ripple.
  All three glow in the dark.
- Other players' trails, auras and pet sparkles show when *Others' auras and trails* is on in
  the game's Wisp settings (off by default). The Particles setting thins or hides them all.
- The game now writes a warning to its log when the default font has no glyphs or a sound file
  can't be opened, so a broken font or silent game can be traced.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
