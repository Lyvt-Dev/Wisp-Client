# Wisp Launcher 0.11.1

- New emote: **False Dreams** (500 coins). You rise off the ground as if possessed, head thrown
  back and arms hanging, while its song plays. Eyes drift around you and turn to stare at whoever
  is watching, blinking now and then, and your own view swims, blurred and dark at its edges.
  Moving or jumping ends it early.
- New emote: **Purple Hollow** (1,200 coins). Gather a blue orb over one hand and a red one over
  the other, bring them together into a purple sphere crawling with lightning, and fire it.
- New cape: **Zedix Cape** (400 coins). A neon purple Z on a dark violet cape: its edge pulses
  and sparks, a glow breathes around it, light sweeps across the letter and violet smoke drifts
  behind.
- The emote wheel now shows each locked emote's own price.
- Several Microsoft accounts: the launcher keeps every account you sign in with, and you can
  switch between them on the Account page. Signing in opens Microsoft's page in a launcher
  window; the code at microsoft.com/link is still there as a fallback.
- Settings is reorganised into General, Game, Appearance, Cosmetics & emotes, Accounts, Storage
  and About.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
