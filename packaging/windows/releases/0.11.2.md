# Wisp Launcher 0.11.2

- New emote: **The Rook** (700 coins). Point at the sky and a giant ivory chess rook falls and
  slams down in front of you. A Brilliant "!!" badge pops up over it, a fuse hisses, and the rook
  explodes forward in a roaring fireball, then crumbles away into teal sparks. Other players
  with shared emotes see it too.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
