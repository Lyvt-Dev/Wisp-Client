# Wisp Launcher 0.11.3

25 new client features, all off until you turn them on in **Options → Wisp Settings → Extras**
or on the launcher's Mods page. They only change what you see: servers and other players notice
nothing.

- **Information HUD:** CPS, ping, facing direction, biome, server, speed, clock, in-game time,
  weather, potion effects with time left, held item durability and arrow count.
- **Visual:**
  - **Freelook:** hold Left Alt to look around your character without turning it.
  - **Always Day:** the sky and light stay at noon.
  - **Item Physics:** dropped items lie flat on the ground.
  - **Arrow Trails:** flying arrows leave sparks behind them.
  - **Target Crosshair:** the crosshair turns red on a mob or player.
- **Combat:**
  - **Health Indicators:** health over mobs and players.
  - **Damage Numbers:** rising damage and healing numbers.
  - **TNT Timer:** the seconds left on lit TNT.
  - **Hit Info:** the reach of your last hit and your combo.
  - **Item Highlighter:** dropped items glow in their rarity's colour.
- **Utility:**
  - **Chat Highlights:** messages naming you get a gold bar and a ping.
  - **Chat Peek:** hold H to see the chat history.
  - **Waypoints:** `/wp add <name>`, `remove`, `list` and `clear` mark places, shown through walls
    with their distance.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
