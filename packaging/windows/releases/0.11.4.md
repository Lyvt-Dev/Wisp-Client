# Wisp Launcher 0.11.4

- **Inventory Search:** a search box over your inventory and chests. Type part of an item's
  name and everything that doesn't match is darkened. Turn it on in **Options → Wisp Settings →
  Extras** (Utility) or on the launcher's Mods page.
- **HUD Editor:** **Extras → HUD Editor: Open...** in a world. Drag the information panel and
  Armor Status anywhere on the screen, scroll over one to make the panels bigger or smaller, and
  pick a text colour and background opacity. Right click a panel to put it back in its corner.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
