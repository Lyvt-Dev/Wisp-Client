# Wisp Launcher 0.12.0

- **New launcher design:** a dark look with green buttons and a slim icon bar on the left for
  Home, Versions, Mods, Shaders, Resource packs, Servers, Shop, Account and Settings.
- **Top bar:** shows whether the game is ready or running, your coins, your account and how many
  instances are running.
- **Home:** a big **Launch game** button on the banner (its arrow picks the instance), shortcuts
  to every page, and a Latest News grid with updates, shaders and shop items.
- **Friends panel:** shows you and whether you're online or playing, with a hint on inviting
  friends through the pause menu's Friends button.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
