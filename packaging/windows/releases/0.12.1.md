# Wisp Launcher 0.12.1

- **Make it yours:** Settings → Appearance now has an accent colour (8 choices), a background
  (Midnight, Black, Slate, Ocean, Plum), the Home banner artwork and a side panel switch. Changes
  show at once.
- **Jump back in:** Home lists your latest worlds. Click one to start the game right in it.
- **Servers:** your saved servers with their icons. Press **Join** to connect directly.
- **Resource packs:** cards with each pack's icon and description; other files are hidden.
- **Side panel:** daily coins, your instances with a Play button, your equipped cosmetics and
  friends.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
