# Wisp Launcher 0.12.2

- **New Store look:** your coins and owned items at the top, a featured carousel with
  thumbnails that turns by itself, slim category tabs with item counts, and bigger item cards in
  rows of four.
- **Tiers:** every item shows Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Founders, by its price.
- **Clearer buttons:** Buy is dimmed when you can't afford an item yet; owned and equipped items
  are marked on their picture.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
