# Wisp Launcher 0.12.3

- **New Versions page:** the selected instance sits on top with its worlds, enabled mods,
  resource packs and saved servers, a big Launch button and shortcuts to its folder, mods and
  logs.
- **Instance tiles:** every instance is a card with its own artwork, version and mod badges,
  when you last played, a favourite star, Play and a menu. A dashed tile makes a new instance.
- **One toolbar:** search, Vanilla/Modded/Imported tabs, Files present and Favorites filters,
  and sorting, all above the tiles.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
