# Wisp Launcher 0.12.6

- **New Resource packs page:** see which packs are on in the game, in their order, and turn
  packs on or off with one click. Other packs in the folder show as cards with their icon and
  description. Locked while Minecraft runs.
- **New Servers page:** every saved server shows whether it's online, its players, ping, message
  of the day and version, with a big Join button and Refresh. Servers that use an SRV address may
  show no status yet; Join still works.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
