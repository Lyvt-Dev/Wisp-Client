# Wisp Launcher 0.12.8

- **Locker:** a new page with your own skin wearing the cosmetics you own. Pick items to try them
  on, turn the player to see the front, sides and back, then press Equip these to wear the look
  in game. Trails, auras and emotes show in game only.
- **New Home:** a featured server card, your latest worlds to jump back in, and shortcut tiles
  with what each page holds.
- **Featured server Zedix.de:** pinned on Home and Servers with live players and ping. In the
  game's Multiplayer list it stays on top and can't be edited or deleted.
- The side panel no longer lists equipped items; they live in the Locker now.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
