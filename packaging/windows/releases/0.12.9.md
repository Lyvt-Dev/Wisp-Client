# Wisp Launcher 0.12.9

- **Friends:** the right column is now your friends list. Add people by their Minecraft name,
  accept or decline requests, and see who is online in the launcher, playing Wisp or on which
  server. Join a friend's server with one click; offline friends show when they were last seen.
- **Wisp server address:** Settings → General has a field for the Wisp server (friends and
  cosmetics) with a status line that says when it is connected.
- The side panel no longer lists instances.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
