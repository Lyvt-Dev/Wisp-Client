# Wisp Launcher 0.13.0

- **Friends in the game:** press Right Shift and open Friends. Accept or decline requests, add
  someone by typing their name, see where friends play and press Join to go to their server.
  A small notice shows when a friend comes online or sends a request.
- Friends see the server you are really on, even after you switch servers in the game.
- **Better HUD:** widgets by the right or bottom edge grow inward instead of off the screen,
  plates no longer shake when numbers change, Keystrokes keys fade out smoothly, and the
  plates got a cleaner glass edge.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved. Friends need the Wisp server
address (Settings, General) and a Microsoft account.
