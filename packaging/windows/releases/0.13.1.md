# Wisp Launcher 0.13.1

- **New Right Shift menu:** a top bar with search and Edit HUD, tabs along the top (All, HUD,
  Combat, Visual, Utility, General, Friends) and a full-width grid of module tiles. Each tile
  shows its item big, its name and a green Enabled or grey Disabled bar to switch it; click the
  tile for its settings, hover it for what it does.
- Darker, cleaner look like the launcher, green switches, and a short slide in when it opens.
- The menu no longer opens while terrain is still loading, which could freeze the loading.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
