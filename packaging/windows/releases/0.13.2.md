# Wisp Launcher 0.13.2

- **Four new emotes** in the Shop and on the emote wheel (B), which now has ten slots:
  - **Celestial Wings** (1,800 coins): two great wings of starlight unfurl behind you, beat
    slowly and lift you off the ground, then scatter into stardust.
  - **Dark Ascension** (1,400): dark energy creeps in, lifts you with your eyes burning violet,
    spirals round you, gathers in and bursts outward.
  - **L Dance** (450): an L to the forehead, bouncing to a note-block beat, then swap hands.
  - **Bruh** (300): a facepalm, a head shake and a sigh.
- **Wisp Original Cape** (1,000): Wisp's own mark glowing on a deep violet cape, with a
  starfield and a spark circling the mark.
- **Stray Head** (600): a wide conical straw hat with a copper rim, in the new Hats category.
- Other Wisp players see the new emotes too, as with the earlier ones.

Existing instances, login, coins and items are preserved.
