# Wisp Client 0.14.0

The first release of **Wisp Client**.

- **New name and icon:** Wisp Client everywhere (launcher, game, installer, Start menu, Shop),
  with a white feather on black as its icon.
- **Black and white look:** the launcher starts on the black backdrop with the new
  Black & White accent (Settings › Appearance still offers the colour accents); the game's
  menus use the same black and white accents.
- **Four new emotes:** Celestial Wings, Dark Ascension, L Dance and Bruh, on an emote wheel (B)
  with ten slots.
- **New cosmetics:** the Wisp Original Cape (the feather on an animated black cape) and Stray
  Head, a conical straw hat in the new Hats category. Coins are Wisp Coins.
- **Wisp Menu (Right Shift):** search, Edit HUD, tabs and a grid of module tiles, and a
  Friends tab.
