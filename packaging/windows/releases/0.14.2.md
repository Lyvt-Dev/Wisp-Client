# Wisp Client 0.14.2

- **Wavy Capes (Wisp Menu › Visual):** capes bend and wave as players move instead of swinging
  as one stiff board: the hem trails behind when you run and the cape sways gently when you
  stand. Off by default; it works on every player's Mojang cape.
- **Rank prefixes on servers:** name tags, the player list and the sidebar now show the whole
  rank a server gives its players (servers with the TAB plugin and similar). Parts of a rank
  without their own colour used to vanish; item names and lore had the same problem.
