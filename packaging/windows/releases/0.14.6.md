# Wisp Client 0.14.6

- **New emotes, on a second page of the emote wheel** (scroll, or press Q / E, while the wheel
  is open):
  - **Sit** (300 coins): sit down on the ground for as long as you like; move to stand up. The
    mouse turns the camera round you.
  - **Crouch Spam** (250), **Skill Issue** (350), **Too Easy** (400), **Point and Laugh** (450)
    and **Cry About It** (500).
- **Party Hat** (500 coins): a striped party cone with polka dots, a gold fringe and a pom-pom.
- **Linux: the sign-in is kept.** The AppImage no longer asks you to sign in on every start. The
  sign-in is stored encrypted, with its key in your desktop's keyring (GNOME Keyring, KWallet), or
  in a file only you can read where there is no keyring.
- **Linux: no more Wayland FPS cap, if you want.** Settings > Game > "Run through X11 (XWayland)"
  opens the game through XWayland, where frames aren't held to the screen's refresh rate. For an
  uncapped frame rate, also turn VSync off and set Max Framerate to Unlimited in the game's Video
  Settings.
