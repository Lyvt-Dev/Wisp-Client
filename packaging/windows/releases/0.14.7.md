# Wisp Client 0.14.7

- **Voice chat: muted now really means muted.** With the voice chat module on, pressing mute (M)
  used to stop only voice activation: holding push-to-talk or whisper still sent your microphone.
  Now nothing is sent while you are muted, in every mode, as in the original Simple Voice Chat.
- **Voice chat: players who leave disappear** from the voice chat player list.
- **The voice chat module is a download on this release** (`voicechat.amod`). To try it: in the
  launcher, open your instance's Mods page, click "Import .amod" and pick the file. It talks to
  servers that run Simple Voice Chat 2.6 or 2.7 for 26.3. It is still experimental and hasn't been
  tested against a real server yet.
