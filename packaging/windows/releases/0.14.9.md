# Wisp Client 0.14.9

- **A fuller, tidier Home page.** The banner now shows the selected instance on the left with
  its version and when you last played, the Launch button under it, and your worlds, resource
  packs and servers on the right. Spacing is even throughout, Jump back in and Shortcuts are the
  same height, and the latest news starts on the first screen.
- **A cleaner Launch button**: plain white with black type in the Black & White theme.
- **The featured server is green**, with a green Join button.
- **Your servers in the side panel**: your saved servers with live players and ping under the
  daily reward. Click one to join it.
