# Wisp Client 0.15.0

- **The Store in the game.** Press **Y** (or Right Shift, then Store) to buy and wear capes,
  wings, pets, trails, auras and emotes without leaving the game. What you buy shows on you at
  once, and other players see it.
- **Coins live on the Wisp server.** Your Wisp Coins and items are kept on the server, so they are
  the same in the launcher and the game, on every PC, and nobody can give themselves coins. Every
  player starts with 1,100 coins (1,000 welcome gift and today's 100), then 100 every day.
- **Creator codes.** Enter a creator's code in the Store: you get 10 % off everything and wear the
  creator's badge before your name, and the creator earns 10 % of every item you buy.
- **Creator dashboard.** Creators get a Creator tab in the Wisp Menu: their code, how many players
  support them, what those bought and the coins it earned them.
