# Wisp Client 0.15.1

- **Wisp on your phone.** New in Settings: **Phone**. Press **Connect a phone**, open the new
  Wisp app for Android, choose **Get them from my PC** and type the six-digit code. The app copies
  the game files this PC prepared from your Minecraft over your Wi-Fi (about 800 MB), so the phone
  plays the same Minecraft 26.3 as the PC. The files go only to the phone that typed the code,
  five wrong codes close the offer, and it ends by itself after 15 idle minutes. If Windows asks,
  allow Wisp on private networks.
- The Android app is a separate download on the same release page (`Wisp-Client-0.15.1-android-arm64.apk`).
