# Wisp Client 0.16.0

- **A new launcher.** Home, Store, Social, Settings, Account and About share one new look and
  Wisp's own layout, with a new Social panel for friends.
- **A new Store.** It is laid out like Home, and your own skin wears the item you pick: drag to
  turn it, tilt and zoom with the mouse, and animated items play right there.
- **The Wisp Founders Cape** is the first cosmetic of the new line, animated and sharp at 12x.
  The old cosmetics are gone.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.16.0.
