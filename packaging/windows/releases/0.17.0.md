# Wisp Client 0.17.0

- **Wisp's own home screen.** The game no longer opens on vanilla's title: your own background
  picture sits blurred behind the buttons, in the launcher's look.
- **Your figure on the home screen.** Your skin stands on the right under your name tag, wearing
  your cosmetics. It breathes, looks at your mouse and waves hello. Click it to wave back.
- **The Wardrobe.** Try on the skins in your skins folder, pick Classic or Slim and wear one, on
  your Microsoft account or offline. Turn your cosmetics on or off and wear or take off each item.
- **A new Wisp Menu** (Right Shift) with a sidebar, category chips and mod tiles, over the
  blurred world.
- **Sharp icons** everywhere in the home screen, the Wardrobe and the Wisp Menu.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.17.0.
