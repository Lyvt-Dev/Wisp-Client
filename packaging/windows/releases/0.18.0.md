# Wisp Client 0.18.0

- **A warmer launcher.** Your background picture shows bright again instead of under dark
  shades, and the pages in the top bar are plain text.
- **Glass cards.** The featured server, friends, instances, settings and Store cards are
  see-through glass over your picture.
- **Faster server resource packs.** Joining a server with a big pack no longer loads everything
  twice when the server sends its pack again, and checking and unpacking packs runs on many
  threads at once.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.18.0.
