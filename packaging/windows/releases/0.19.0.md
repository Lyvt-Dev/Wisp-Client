# Wisp Client 0.19.0

- **A cleaner in-game HUD**: the widgets are calm, neutral plates now instead of violet glass,
  in the client's black and white. Pressed keys in Keystrokes light up white.
- **Make every widget your own**: your own label text, the format (`FPS 144`, `144 FPS`,
  `[FPS: 144]`, value only...), the font (Minecraft's too), colours from swatches, a hue strip or
  a hex code, rainbow text, the background, the border, corners, padding and alignment.
- **More options per module**: FPS colours and update rate, arrow keys in Keystrokes,
  coordinates with decimals and the Nether position, and more for Health Indicators, Damage
  Numbers and TNT Timer.
- **A live preview** on each widget's page in the Wisp Menu (Right Shift), settings in sections,
  and options that don't apply right now greyed out.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.19.0.
