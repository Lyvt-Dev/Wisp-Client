# Wisp Client 0.21.0

- **The Store works without the Wisp server for now.** Coins, purchases and what you wear are
  kept on your computer, and you start with 10,000 coins to try everything. Creator codes come
  back with the server.
- **Rebuilt cosmetics**, made of solid 3D blocks like the Bamboo Sword:
  - **Demon Wings**: big bat wings with black bones, ivory claws and an ember-red skin. They open
    wide and fold back as they beat.
  - **Angel Wings**: gold arms with rows of white feathers that fan out, opening and closing as
    they beat.
  - **Stray Head**: a stepped conical straw hat with a copper rim and a white knot.
- **New in the Store:**
  - **Lavender** (800 coins): an animated cape of lavender swaying on a green hill under a
    summer sky, with drifting clouds and a butterfly.
  - **Devil Horns** (400 coins): two curved crimson horns.
  - **Angel Halo** (400 coins): a glowing gold ring floating over your head.
- The Store shows wings and other animated items moving, its categories are cleaner tabs, and
  the rarity labels are gone.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.21.0.
