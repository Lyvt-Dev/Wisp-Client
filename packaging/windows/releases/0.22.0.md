# Wisp Client 0.22.0

- **Wisp Settings is tidier.** Options › Wisp Settings now groups its buttons under headings:
  Interface, Graphics, Controls and players, Mods and online, and AI.
- **Menu style: Wisp or Minecraft.** The first button under Interface switches the title screen
  between Wisp's home screen (your skin and your own backgrounds) and Minecraft's classic title
  screen with the panorama. Singleplayer, Multiplayer and Options work the same in both.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.22.0.
