# Wisp Client 0.23.0

- **Wisp Christmas.** Options › Wisp Settings › Menu style now has three looks: Wisp, Wisp
  Christmas and Minecraft. Wisp Christmas puts snow, twinkling fairy lights and a Santa hat on
  Wisp's feather on the home screen and in the Wisp Menu (Right Shift), with red buttons and
  "Merry Christmas from Wisp".
- **New in the Store:**
  - **Santa Hat** (500 coins): red velvet with a white fur brim, its tip flopping over to one
    side with a pompom that swings as you move.
  - **Santa's Sack** (700 coins): a big burlap sack slung over your back, tied with gold rope,
    with a present and a candy cane poking out. It is the first item in the new **Bags** tab and
    can be worn together with a cape.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.23.0.
