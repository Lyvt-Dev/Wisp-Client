# Wisp Client 0.24.0

- **15 new items in the Store:**
  - **Wings:** **Void Dragon Wings** (1,000 coins), near-black bone with a violet skin glowing
    lilac at the edge, and **Sakura Wings** (1,000 coins), pink feathers dotted with cherry
    blossoms.
  - **Weapon:** **Crimson Katana** (600 coins), a red lacquered scabbard with gold fittings
    across your back.
  - **Hats:** Gearwork Topper (500), Spelunker Helmet (400), Frost Wizard Hat (500), Tangerine
    Topper (400), Panda Cap (400), Buccaneer Tricorn (500), Orbit Cap (600, planets circling
    your head), Propeller Cap (400, the propeller spins) and Neon Headset (500).
  - **Glasses**, a new tab: **Blossom Shades** (400 coins), pink flower-shaped glasses you can
    wear together with any hat.
  - **Aura:** **Butterfly Swarm** (800 coins), six butterflies fluttering round you.
  - **Pet:** **Astro Buddy** (900 coins), a little astronaut floating by your shoulder.
- The Santa Hat's brim no longer flickers against your skin's hat layer, and Santa's Sack is
  redrawn in pixel art at Minecraft's own scale.
- When Minecraft sign-in is refused, the message now says what the Minecraft services answered.
- The launcher updates itself to 0.24.0.
